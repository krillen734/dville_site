Office & Waste Collection Holidays

City Hall and Waste-Pro collection both observe the following 2026 holidays.

City Hall Closed

  • New Year's Day, Thursday, January 1
  • Good Friday, Friday, April 3
  • Memorial Day, Monday, May 25
  • Independence Day, Friday, July 3 (observed)
  • Labor Day, Monday, September 7
  • Thanksgiving, Thursday & Friday, November 26–27
  • Christmas Eve & Day, Thursday & Friday, December 24–25

Waste-Pro Collection Suspended

  • New Year's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • July 4th
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Christmas Day

Collection resumes on the next scheduled pickup day following a holiday suspension.

Water Rates

Fees & Rates

Water and utility account fees set by the city.

Late fee
$25.00 — applied on the 16th if payment isn't received by the 15th
Reconnection (1st offense)
$75.00 for service disconnected due to non-payment
Reconnection (subsequent)
$100.00 for each additional reconnection
Returned check fee
$35.00 for any returned check
Business Licensing

Operating a Business in Danielsville

Businesses operating within city limits are required to hold a current business license. The fee for a Regular Business License is $100 per year.

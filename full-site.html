Office & Waste Collection Holidays

City Hall and Waste-Pro collection both observe the following 2026 holidays.

City Hall Closed

  • New Year's Day, Thursday, January 1
  • Good Friday, Friday, April 3
  • Memorial Day, Monday, May 25
  • Independence Day, Friday, July 3 (observed)

  • Memorial Day
  • July 4th

  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Christmas Day

Due Dates & Late Fees

Bill due
15th of each month
Payment deadline
Before 3:00pm on the 15th
Late fee
$25 applied after the deadline
Avoid the late fee Payments must be received before 3:00pm on the 15th of the month. Payments made after that time, even on the same day, are subject to the $25 late fee.

Water Rates

Fees & Rates

Water and utility account fees set by the city.

Late fee
$25.00, applied on the 16th if payment isn't received by the 15th
Reconnection (1st offense)
$75.00 for service disconnected due to non-payment
Reconnection (subsequent)
$100.00 for each additional reconnection
Returned check fee
$35.00 for any returned check
Business Licensing

Operating a Business in Danielsville

Businesses operating within city limits are required to hold a current business license. The fee for a Regular Business License is $100 per year.
